Publications

  • 2024
    A Linear Analysis of Torsional Alfvén Waves in Open Twisted Divergent Magnetic Flux Tubes for Coronal Heating
    Solar Physics

    A linear analysis of torsional Alfvén wave behavior and energy transfer in an open, twisted, divergent magnetic flux tube, showing that intermediate wave frequencies most efficiently transmit energy to heat the solar corona.

  • 2025
    A Critical Review on Turbulent Energy Dissipation Mechanism in Solar Corona to Astrophysical Systems and a Physics-Informed Neural Network Approach
    Solar Physics

    A review of turbulent energy dissipation mechanisms in MHD systems relevant to coronal heating, synthesizing classical turbulence theories and evaluating physics-informed neural networks as an emerging modeling approach.

    CRC Press (Applied Research in Engineering Sciences)

    Investigates the generation of compressible acoustic waves through Alfvén wave mode coupling in the solar corona.

  • 2026
    Parametric Decay Instability as a Source of Turbulence in an Open Leakage Permitting Vertical Solar Coronal Domain
    Solar Physics

    A study of parametric decay instability as a turbulence-generating mechanism in an open, leakage-permitting vertical solar coronal domain.

    Journal of the Korean Astronomical Society

I am an Assistant Professor of Physics at Sikkim Government Science College, Chakung, and a PhD Scholar at the Sikkim Manipal Institute of Technology. My research interests include solar physics, magnetohydrodynamics, Alfvén waves, compressible plasma turbulence, and coronal heating. I am passionate about research, teaching, and developing computational approaches to understanding plasma processes in the solar atmosphere.

My broader interests lie in solar physics, computational astrophysics, and MHD wave theory.
